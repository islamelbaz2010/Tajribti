## Summary

Tajribti is a technology-enabled Consumer Intelligence Platform targeting Egypt's FMCG, beauty, and pharma-OTC sectors. The business model is B2B2C: brands pay for structured consumer data; consumers receive free product samples in exchange for post-trial survey feedback. The platform converts every physical product trial into a brand-actionable consumer data point. The name "Tajribti" (تجربتي) means "my experience" in Arabic.

---

## Mission & Vision

**Mission:** Replace guesswork in product launches with real-time, consented consumer truth — starting with Egypt's FMCG, beauty, and pharma-OTC sectors.

**Vision:** To become the default consumer-intelligence layer behind every new product launch in Egypt within 3 years, and across MENA within 5–7 years, contingent on Egypt unit economics being proven first.

**North Star Metric:** Verified, brand-actionable consumer data points delivered per month.

---

## What It Is NOT

Per the Investment Committee and Founder Decisions Document, Tajribti is explicitly **not**:
- A sampling company
- A promotional activation agency
- A field marketing company
- A logistics operation
- A consumer-growth-hacking company

It is a **data company that uses free samples as its consumer acquisition channel**.

---

## Reference Company

**Samplia** (Barcelona/Madrid, Spain) — Founded 2013. Bootstrapped, no institutional funding. ~40–50M samples distributed, 300–400+ brands served, ~2M app users. A proven services business, not a venture-scale outcome. The Egypt model is a redesigned, Egypt-first version — not a literal copy.

---

## Core Values

1. Evidence over opinion — every internal decision cites data or is labeled a bet
2. Consent is non-negotiable — no dark patterns in data collection, ever
3. Speed beats polish — ship the smallest thing that answers a real question
4. Data is the product, not the exhaust
5. Egypt-first, not Egypt-adapted

## Business Model Summary

| Layer | Detail |
|---|---|
| Customer (paying) | FMCG / Beauty / Pharma-OTC brand marketing teams |
| Consumer (data source) | Egyptian consumers aged 18–40, Cairo/Giza/Alexandria |
| Core mechanic | Physical product trial + instant post-trial survey |
| Revenue | Campaign fees, per-sample fees, AI dashboard subscription, panel access, Enterprise API |
| Moat | Proprietary first-party consumer dataset + brand relationships |
