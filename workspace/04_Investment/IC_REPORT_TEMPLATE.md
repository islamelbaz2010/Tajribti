## Problem Statement (from Report)

**Problem for brands:**
- Samples distributed randomly — no data on who received, tried, liked, or repurchased
- Traditional market research is slow (weeks)
- Brands need feedback within hours of product trial, not a full research cycle
- Poor ROI measurement on sampling spend

**Brand customers identified:**
Nestlé, Coca-Cola, PepsiCo, Unilever, P&G, L'Oréal, Mondelez, and any company launching new products.

---

## Platform Solution Architecture (Original Reconstruction)

```
Brand ← requests sampling campaign
  ← Platform defines target segment
  ← Selects locations
  ← Consumer receives product
  ← Consumer tries product
  ← Consumer completes survey
  ← Brand receives dashboard + analytics
```

**Value added over traditional sampling:**
- Every sample linked to a verified consumer identity
- Measures: age, gender, city, interests, purchase rate, opinion, repurchase probability
- Real-time feedback (hours, not weeks)
- Attributable ROI
