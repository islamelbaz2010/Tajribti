How a TAJRIBTI campaign works

From the product on a shelf to a decision on your desk — in one connected pipeline.

1 · Trial

Real consumers try your product

Consumers discover your campaign or scan its QR code, answer eligibility screening, and receive the product to try in real conditions.

2 · Feedback

Structured feedback after the trial

After trying the product, consumers answer the campaign's survey — ratings, purchase intent, campaign-specific questions and open verbatims.

3 · Evidence

Evidence becomes a decision-ready report

Every response is persisted and measured live — funnel, purchase intent, audience differences and findings land in a report your team can act on.

Finding

Purchase intent averaged 3.8/5 across 184 responses; taste met expectations for 121 of 184.

Insight

Response is positive overall but not uniform — female and Cairo segments rate highest at this sample size.

Decision implication

Directional support to proceed — weighted toward the stronger segments — pending a larger confirmatory wave.

Recommendation

Review purchase intent (3.8/5, n=184) and product experience (4.1/5, n=179) together before drawing any conclusion.

Questions companies ask

How do consumers take part?

Through the TAJRIBTI mobile app — they enter a campaign, pass eligibility screening, try the product, then answer the post-trial survey. There is no consumer web login.

Who uses the web workspaces?

Company teams (admins and read-only members) and TAJRIBTI Operations. One staff login routes each account to the right workspace.

What does a report contain?

The participation funnel, purchase intent, product-experience rating, campaign-question breakdowns, consumer verbatims, findings and recommendations — each figure with its sample size.

Where do the numbers come from?

Only from persisted participation and survey records for that campaign — computed live, never estimated or AI-generated. Figures below the minimum evidence threshold are suppressed, not smoothed over.
