## 6. DATA AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**Brand data ownership:** [BRAND NAME] owns all consumer data collected during their campaign. Tajribti does not resell or share brand-specific campaign data with any third party.

**Platform IP:** The Tajribti platform, survey engine, analytics methodology, and consumer intelligence report format remain the exclusive property of Tajribti.

**Aggregated data:** Tajribti may use anonymised, aggregated, non-brand-attributable data to improve the platform. No data that could identify [BRAND NAME] or their products will be used without explicit written consent.

**Consumer consent:** All consumer data is collected under explicit opt-in consent at point of trial. Tajribti is responsible for the consent mechanism. [BRAND NAME] confirms their campaign complies with applicable Egyptian consumer protection and data privacy regulations.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to keep the terms of this letter, and any commercial information shared during the campaign, confidential. This obligation survives the end of the pilot.

---

## 8. NON-EXCLUSIVITY

This letter of intent does not grant [BRAND NAME] exclusivity within their product category. Tajribti may work with other brands across all categories unless a separate exclusivity agreement is negotiated and signed.

---

## 9. TERMINATION

Either party may withdraw from this letter of intent with 7 days written notice before campaign launch. After campaign launch, withdrawal terms will be governed by the formal campaign agreement.

If Tajribti is unable to deliver the agreed consumer volume within the agreed timeline, Tajribti will provide a prorated refund on the campaign fee proportional to the shortfall.
