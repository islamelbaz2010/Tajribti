## What This Project Is

Tajribti (تجربتي — "my experience") is a pre-launch Consumer Intelligence Platform for Egypt's FMCG, beauty, and pharma-OTC sectors. It operates as a B2B2C platform: brands pay for structured consumer data; consumers receive free product samples in exchange for post-trial survey feedback.

The reference model is Samplia (Spain, founded 2013, bootstrapped), which demonstrates the concept works but is a services-scale business, not a venture-scale outcome.

## Constraints Every AI Must Know

1. **Development is not authorized** — do not suggest or generate production engineering work as if it is ready to start
2. **All financial figures are illustrative** — never present them as validated forecasts
3. **"Tajribti" is provisional** — not a confirmed brand name; treat with naming caution
4. **Samplia is a services business** — temper any venture-scale growth comparisons
5. **Marketeers Research is a real competitor** — do not repeat the error of claiming "no direct Egyptian competitor"
6. **PDPL is a gate, not a risk to accept** — data-collecting features cannot ship without legal sign-off
7. **Cairo only in Year 1** — do not suggest geographic expansion before unit economics are proven
