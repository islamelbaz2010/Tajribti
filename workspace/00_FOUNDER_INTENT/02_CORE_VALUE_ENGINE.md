This is the only chain that creates value. Challenge every step added beyond it.

```
Consumer
↓

↓
Tries the product   [always happens — this step cannot fail]
↓
Answers 5 questions on a phone
↓
Brand receives consumer intelligence report within 24 hours
↓

```

**Every feature in the platform must strengthen at least one step in this chain.**  
**Every feature that strengthens zero steps must be postponed.**

## WHAT IS ACTUALLY BEING SOLD

Tajribti is NOT selling technology, software access, or platform subscriptions.

**Tajribti is selling a question answered.**

The question every brand marketing director needs answered after a sampling campaign:

> *"What did Egyptian consumers actually think of my product the first time they tried it?"*

The answer to that question is worth money. It is worth money because:
1. Traditional research takes 3–6 weeks. Tajribti delivers in 24 hours.
2. Traditional research uses recall panels. Tajribti captures feedback at the moment of first experience, before any purchase decision.
3. Brands cannot produce this data internally without building a platform company — which they will never do.

**Sell the answer. Not the technology.**

*Source: `07_Product/GO_TO_MARKET.md` — Sales Message (What Brands Buy)*

## THE COMPETITIVE MOAT

The moat is NOT the mobile app. Any developer can build a QR + survey app.

The moat is the **Egyptian consumer panel** — a registered, consented, profiled database of consumers who have completed verified product trials.

After 10 campaigns: 5,000 profiles.  
After 50 campaigns: 50,000 profiles.  
After 100 campaigns: the panel is too expensive for any competitor to replicate.

The first company to build this panel in Egypt owns the market.  
**That race has already started. Start the clock.**
