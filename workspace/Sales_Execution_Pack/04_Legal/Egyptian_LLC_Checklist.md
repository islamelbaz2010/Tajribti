# EGYPTIAN LLC INCORPORATION — FOUNDER CHECKLIST

**Blocker:** B-02 — Must close before Sprint 0 vendor contracts can be signed.  
**Entity type:** Limited Liability Company (LLC) — to convert to Joint Stock Company (JSC) as company scales.  
**Authority:** Companies Law No. 159 of 1981 (as amended); Investment Law No. 72 of 2017.  
**Source:** `04_Investment/IC_MEMO_v1.0.md` · `15_Decisions/OPEN_DECISIONS_TRACKER.md` · `AI_BOOTSTRAP/02_PROJECT_STATE.md`

> **Note:** Steps and timelines below reflect the standard GAFI one-stop-shop process as of 2026. Verify current requirements with a licensed Egyptian corporate lawyer before proceeding. Do not rely on this checklist as legal advice.

---

## WHAT CLOSES THIS BLOCKER

One of two outcomes closes B-02:
- A commercial register number (Sijil Tijari) in hand, OR
- A signed formation agreement with a confirmed formation date

Either outcome allows Sprint 0 vendor contracts (AWS, WhatsApp BSP, SMS provider, payment rails) to be signed.

---

## PRE-REGISTRATION DECISIONS

Complete these before visiting any authority.

- [ ] **Confirm company name.** "Tajribti" is a provisional working name. Check trademark and domain clearance before registering. Provisional name is usable for LOIs — update legal name in formal contracts after registration.
- [ ] **Confirm legal structure.** LLC (Sharikat Tawsiya Basita or Sharikat Masouliyet Mahdouda). Minimum 1 founder. No minimum capital requirement for LLCs under Law 72/2017.
- [ ] **Confirm registered address.** Must be a physical Cairo address. Can be a serviced office or business center address.
- [ ] **Confirm activity code.** Technology / software company + data services. Your lawyer will confirm the correct GAFI activity classification.
- [ ] **Decide shareholder structure.** If you are the sole founder: 100% ownership. If co-founder or investor is involved: agree ownership percentages before registration.

## STEP-BY-STEP CHECKLIST

### Phase 1 — Name Reservation (1–2 days)

- [ ] Visit GAFI One-Stop-Shop (6 October City or Nile City Tower — Cairo branch) **or** use GAFI's online portal (investment.gov.eg)
- [ ] Submit name reservation request for "Tajribti" (or confirmed final name)
- [ ] Receive name approval certificate
- [ ] **If name is taken:** prepare 3 alternative names in advance

---

### Phase 2 — Document Preparation (3–5 days — engage a lawyer)

- [ ] Draft Memorandum of Association (MOA) — defines company activity, capital, ownership, management
- [ ] Prepare founder National ID (or passport if non-Egyptian)
- [ ] Prepare proof of registered address (lease or service agreement for office)
- [ ] Prepare articles of incorporation (your lawyer drafts this)
- [ ] Notarize MOA with a licensed Egyptian notary

---

### Phase 3 — GAFI Registration (1–3 working days)

- [ ] Submit complete registration package to GAFI One-Stop-Shop
- [ ] Pay registration fees (variable — confirm current fees with GAFI or your lawyer)
- [ ] Receive company registration certificate from GAFI
- [ ] Receive Commercial Register number (Sijil Tijari) — **this closes B-02**

---

### Phase 4 — Post-Registration (1–2 weeks, run in parallel)

- [ ] Obtain Tax Registration Card (Bitaqa Darebeya) from Egyptian Tax Authority
- [ ] Register for VAT if applicable (consult your accountant — threshold-based)
- [ ] Register employees with the Social Insurance Authority (after first hire)
- [ ] Open corporate bank account — bring Commercial Register + Tax Card + MOA + founder ID
  - Recommended banks: CIB Egypt, HSBC Egypt, Banque Misr, QNB Alahli (confirm current business account requirements)
- [ ] Obtain trade practice certificate if required for your activity code
- [ ] Register company seal if required for contract execution

---

### Phase 5 — Enable Sprint 0 Activity (immediately after Phase 3)

- [ ] Update LOI template with confirmed legal entity name and registration number
- [ ] Share Commercial Register number with AWS for AWS account setup
- [ ] Share with WhatsApp Business Solution Provider for BSP contract
- [ ] Share with InstaPay / Vodafone Cash for payment rail integration (Sprint 1+)
- [ ] Update all draft vendor agreements with legal entity name
