## Summary

Tajribti is a B2B-first business. The go-to-market is sequenced: secure brand supply before building consumer demand. Without brand clients there are no campaigns; without campaigns there are no consumers; without consumers brands will not renew. Brand supply is the constraint that unlocks everything else.

---

## Critical GTM Insight

> Build supply before demand.

This is not a consumer product that can grow virally. Consumer-side marketing exists to acquire the data panel, not to build brand awareness. Brand-side growth comes from Sales- and CS-generated case studies and referrals, not paid advertising.

## GTM Sequence

| Stage | Focus | Success Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 — Brand Supply | Secure first 3–5 signed brand pilots before building anything | 3 paid pilots committed |
| Stage 2 — First Campaigns | Run first campaigns in Cairo with the 3–5 pilot brands | First usable campaign report delivered |
| Stage 3 — Consumer Panel | Build consumer panel through targeted Cairo consumer acquisition | Statistical minimum panel size achieved |
| Stage 4 — Data Flywheel | Panel grows → data quality improves → brand WTP increases → reinvest | First brand renewal |
| Stage 5 — Cairo Scale | Add more brands, more locations, more consumer segments | Break-even trajectory confirmed |
| Stage 6 — Geographic Expansion | Alexandria, Giza, New Cairo, 6th October | Cairo unit economics proven |
| Stage 7 — Regional | GCC — only after Egypt is proven | Hard gate — not a calendar date |

### Sales Message (What Brands Buy)

**Don't sell:**
- Software
- A sampling service
- Data access

**Sell:**
- Measurable product trial outcomes
- Proof that $X in campaign spend equals Y data points from Z demographics
- A defensible ROI story for the marketing director's management
- Speed to insight (hours vs. weeks vs. traditional research)

### Kill Criterion (from IC Memo)

> *"If you don't get 5 interested companies in the discovery phase, don't start coding."*

This is the quantitative gate for Track 0. If 5 named brands will not commit to a pilot, the commercial thesis is unproven.

### Consumer Acquisition Channels

| Channel | Purpose | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Social media (Instagram, TikTok) | Consumer app download + campaign awareness | From first brand pilot |
| In-location marketing at campaign sites | Drive app installs at point of experience | From first campaign |
| Referral program | Peer-invite with rewards incentive | V1 (post-MVP) |
| Push notifications | Drive repeat visits to active campaigns | From MVP |

### Consumer Onboarding Design Principle

> Friction is the enemy. Every unnecessary tap costs completed surveys — and completed surveys are the actual product.

Goal: consumer app registration + first campaign redemption + survey completion in under 5 minutes total.

## Competitive Differentiation

### What makes Tajribti different from Marketeers Research?

Marketeers Research sells analytics for FMCG pricing/promotion/assortment decisions using survey panels. Tajribti converts physical product trial (in-the-moment experience) into instant structured data. The differentiation is on the **sampling-to-data pipeline**, not on analytics alone.

The question every brand prospect will ask: *"Why can't Marketeers Research do this?"*

**The answer:** Because Marketeers Research does not operate a physical sampling mechanic. They survey people who have already bought a product. Tajribti captures feedback at the moment of first experience — before any purchase decision. That's a fundamentally different (and more valuable) data point.
