## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Your only goal in the next 60 days is three signed pilot LOIs.

You do not need a product. You do not need a team. You need three brand marketing directors to say yes to a pilot agreement.

Every conversation you have should end with one of three outcomes: a booked discovery call, a sent LOI, or a clear no. Anything else is noise.

**Kill criterion (from IC Memo):** If you do not reach 5 interested companies at the discovery stage, do not start building. Three LOIs = commercial thesis proven. Fewer than 3 = pivot or pause.

## FOUNDER POSITIONING

**How to introduce yourself. Use this consistently.**

You are the founder of Tajribti — Egypt's Consumer Intelligence Platform.

Tajribti converts physical product trials into structured, brand-actionable consumer data within 24 hours. Brands get the data they need to make launch decisions — faster and with more certainty than traditional market research.

**What you are not.** A sampling company. An activation agency. A promotional event operator. A coupon app. If a brand describes you as any of those, correct it immediately and gently.

**What you are.** A Consumer Intelligence company that uses product trials as the data collection method.

**Never lead with the technology.** Lead with the outcome: faster decisions, measurable results, defensible ROI.

## VALUE PROPOSITION

**What brands actually buy. Say this, not the tech version.**

Source: `07_Product/GO_TO_MARKET.md` — "Sales Message (What Brands Buy)"

**Sell these three things:**

**1. Measurable results**
Every campaign produces a consumer data report: who tried the product, what they thought, how likely they are to buy, broken down by demographic. Not impressions. Not reach. Data.

**2. Speed**
Results within 24 hours of campaign end. Traditional market research takes 3 to 6 weeks. By then, your launch decision has already been made without data.

**3. A story you can take upstairs**
Your marketing director needs to justify sampling spend to leadership. We give you numbers. $X in campaign spend produced Y consumer data points from Z demographics. That's a defensible ROI narrative.

**Do not sell:** Software. Platform access. Data subscriptions. Those are how we package it internally. Brands buy results.

## COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATION

**You will be compared to Marketeers Research. Be ready.**

Source: `07_Product/GO_TO_MARKET.md` · `12_Reviews/PEER_REVIEW_MASTER_REPORT.md`

**The question you will hear:** "How are you different from Marketeers Research?"

**The answer:**

Marketeers Research surveys people who have already bought your product. They capture post-purchase opinion — useful, but limited.

Tajribti captures feedback at the moment of first experience, before any purchase decision has been made. A consumer tries your product for the first time, and within minutes they tell you what they actually think — not what they remember thinking two weeks later.

That is a fundamentally different data point. In-moment, pre-purchase, first-impression data is what brand innovation teams actually need to validate whether a product deserves national distribution.

**Other comparisons:**

| Compared to | Key difference |
|---|---|
| Traditional sampling campaigns | Those produce reach numbers. We produce data. |
| BTL activation agencies | They manage events. We deliver consumer intelligence reports. |
| Paid survey platforms | They survey panels with no product interaction. We capture feedback linked to an actual trial moment. |
| Doing nothing (status quo) | No data on sampling ROI at all. We change that. |

## DISCOVERY CALL AGENDA

**30 minutes. Four stages. Do not run longer.**

| Stage | Duration | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1 — Open** | 3 min | Establish credibility, set agenda |
| **Stage 2 — Qualify** | 10 min | Understand their situation and pain |
| **Stage 3 — Pitch** | 10 min | Present the solution to their specific pain |
| **Stage 4 — Close** | 7 min | Agree next step — send LOI or book follow-up |

---

**Stage 1 — Open (3 minutes)**

"Thank you for your time. In the next 30 minutes I want to understand how your brand currently validates new products with consumers, show you what Tajribti does, and then decide together whether a pilot makes sense. Does that work for you?"

---

**Stage 2 — Qualify (10 minutes)**

Listen more than you talk. Take notes. These answers are your pitch.

**Go through the Discovery Questions section below.**

---

**Stage 3 — Pitch (10 minutes)**

Do not use slides. Talk through the following sequence:

1. Reflect back what you heard: "You said X is your main challenge. Here's how we solve that specifically."
2. Explain the platform in two sentences: "Tajribti puts branded product samples at physical retail locations in Cairo. Each trial is linked to a consumer profile. After the trial, the consumer completes a short survey on their phone. Within 24 hours, you receive a report."
3. Show the differentiation: "Traditional research would give you this same data in 3 to 6 weeks, from people who recall a product they tried weeks ago. We capture the reaction in the moment."
4. Quantify: "A pilot campaign produces data from [X] consumers, broken down by demographic profile, purchase intent score, and open-ended feedback on your specific product attributes."

---

**Stage 4 — Close (7 minutes)**

"Based on what you've shared, I think a pilot would be a good fit. Our pilots run for [30 or 60 days], with [number] consumers from [their target demographic]. The commercial terms are simple — I can send you a one-page letter of intent for your review. Does that make sense as a next step?"

**If yes:** Send LOI within 24 hours. See LOI Handover Process below.
**If "let us think about it":** See Objection Response O-03 below.
**If no:** Ask why. Log the reason in CRM. Thank them.

## DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

**Ask these in order. Do not skip.**

1. "How does your brand currently validate whether a new product resonates with consumers before a national launch?"

2. "Are you running sampling campaigns today? If yes, what data do you get back from them?"

3. "How long does it typically take to get consumer feedback from a traditional research study?"

4. "When you present sampling results to your management, what do they ask you to prove?"

5. "What would a successful pilot need to show you for it to become a full-year engagement?"

6. "Who else on your team would need to be involved in a decision like this?"

7. "Is there a product launch in the next 90 days that this could be relevant to?"

8. "Have you worked with Marketeers Research or similar? What was useful — and what wasn't?"

9. "What's your biggest frustration with how consumer data is collected today?"

10. "If we could solve [the pain they named], what would that be worth to your brand annually?"

## PRICING DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

**Do not name a price first. Understand their budget first.**

1. "What does your brand currently spend annually on consumer research and sampling?"

2. "For a single sampling campaign in Cairo, what does your current budget look like?"

3. "Is consumer research budgeted separately from sampling, or from the same line?"

4. "If a pilot produced the consumer data report I described, what would a reasonable cost look like from your side?"

5. "What would you need to see from a pilot to justify scaling to 3 to 4 campaigns per year?"

**Pricing guidance** *(from `04_Investment/INVESTMENT_DUE_DILIGENCE_REPORT_v2.md` — illustrative, unvalidated):*
Pilot campaigns are expected in the $4,000–$20,000 range depending on scope and consumer volume. Do not anchor to the top of this range in the first conversation. Let the brand's stated budget inform where you open.

## TOP OBJECTIONS AND RESPONSES

**Three objections you will hear. Know these before every call.**

---

**O-01: "We already work with Marketeers Research."**

"That makes sense — they're a solid research operation. What they do well is analyzing what consumers think about products they've already purchased. What Tajribti captures is different: the first-impression reaction at the moment of trial, before any purchase decision. That's data Marketeers Research doesn't collect, because they don't operate a physical sampling mechanic. The two can actually work alongside each other — we capture the trial moment, they can handle the post-purchase longitudinal analysis."

---

**O-02: "Our sampling budget is already allocated for this year."**

"Understood. Many brands we've spoken with categorize this as a consumer research budget rather than a sampling budget — because the deliverable is a data report, not a promotional event. It might be worth checking whether it sits differently in your planning. And for the pilot specifically, we're designing the scope to fit within a single campaign investment — not an annual commitment. We can structure it to be testable within whatever flexibility you have."

---

**O-03: "Interesting — let us think about it / we need to review internally."**

"Of course, I appreciate that. A few things that might help your internal conversation: I can send you a one-page brief that explains the platform in non-technical terms for your colleagues. I can also share the letter of intent so you can see the terms before deciding — there's no obligation in reviewing it. And if it helps, I can speak with whoever else needs to be involved. What would be most useful for you right now?"

**Say this when the conversation reaches agreement. Word for word.**

"Great — I think we're aligned. Here's what I'll do: I'll send you the one-page brand brief and a draft letter of intent today. The LOI outlines the pilot scope, timeline, and commercial terms. There are no surprises — it's designed to be simple to review. Take a look, come back to me with any changes, and we'll go from there. Sound good?"

We set up the logistics at the retail location, run the campaign, collect the data, and deliver your consumer intelligence report within 24 hours of campaign end. The whole pilot takes [30 or 60] days from start to report."

## LOI HANDOVER PROCESS

**Steps after a brand agrees to review the LOI.**

1. Complete the LOI template (`03_LOI_Template.md`) with their brand name, agreed pilot scope, and proposed dates.
2. Send by email — subject line: "Tajribti Pilot LOI — [Brand Name]"
3. Body: "Please find attached the letter of intent for your pilot campaign. The terms are straightforward — one page. Happy to walk through any questions on a quick call."
4. Attach the completed LOI as a PDF or share as a Google Doc link.
5. Log the LOI date in MEOS LOI tab: columns A–F (LOI ID, Account ID, Company Name, Contact, Type, Issued Date).
6. Follow up on Day 3 if no response (WhatsApp).
7. When signed: in LOI tab Column I (Brand Countersigned?), select ✅ Yes from the dropdown — do not type free text. If a countersign date column exists adjacent to Column I, log the date there. Mark CRM Stage as "Stage 5 — LOI."
8. Notify yourself in Dashboard Kill Criterion Status. Count toward the 3-LOI target.
